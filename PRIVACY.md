# Privacy — joinprojectsol.org concept preview

The current public website is a static preview without user accounts, a chatbot, a shared database, or a contact-submission service.

The First-Step Studio creates a guided plan **within your browser** from the information you type. The website's scripts do not send that idea to an AI provider or to a Project SOL server. You may choose to copy or download the plan yourself.

The website uses a service worker to cache public site files for faster and offline access. The host and the services reached when following external links may maintain their own access logs and privacy practices.

Future accounts, directories, applications, or forms must receive a dedicated privacy review before launch. Please do not submit private or sensitive information in any future public feature without understanding how it will be handled.
